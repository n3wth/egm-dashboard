Privacy

Boing’s privacy notice is being prepared. This page describes the status of that work and is not a complete privacy notice.

The planned workspace

The planned beta uses private workspaces for project information. Workspace owners will decide whether to enable optional AI guidance after reviewing how that feature processes data.

Public signup and login are not yet available. The service and its data practices are still being finalized.

What the notice will cover

The reviewed notice will identify the operator and privacy contact, explain which account and project information is collected, and describe its purposes, service providers, retention periods, and processing locations.

It will also explain applicable privacy rights, data access and deletion requests, cookies and similar technologies, and the processing involved in optional AI guidance.

Before you connect data

The completed notice and terms must be available before public onboarding. Details about providers, retention, and privacy requests remain pending and should not be inferred from this preview.
