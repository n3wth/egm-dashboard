Repository and website, together.

The planned project view brings GitHub activity and website performance into a shared place for review.

  • Keep the repository and website associated with their project.
  • Review the available measurements from each connected source.
  • Keep the reporting window visible when interpreting traffic.

See the source behind the signal.

Boing is designed to show where a measurement came from and when it was collected. That context helps you judge whether it answers your question.

  • Check the age of collected information.
  • Recognize unavailable sources and collection failures.
  • Distinguish missing measurements from measured values.

A place for your projects.

The planned beta uses private organization workspaces. Members will work with the projects they are authorized to access.

Workspace access, project exports, and deletion are part of the launch validation. Public onboarding will remain closed until those checks and the legal notices are complete.

Read the privacy notice status

Guidance, when you choose it.

Optional AI guidance is planned to help you explore possible next steps using workspace data.

The intended setup leaves AI disabled until a workspace owner enables it after reviewing the processing disclosures. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect and need your review.

This feature is still in development. Provider and data-processing details will be published before the beta opens.

Built around your review.

Boing’s goal is to make project information easier to understand, so you can choose your next action.
